# Three-minute SHIFT Adaptive demonstration

Use the local demo. Weather is simulated; demo personas are fictional role switches.

1. **0:00–0:25 — Operations:** show Overview and the team calendar. Explain actual
   free windows, confirmed work, and private busy intervals. Open a project/task.
2. **0:25–0:55 — Disruption:** open SHIFT Adaptive → Eastside. Simulate heavy rain.
   Point to source/update labels and the electrical task's configured threshold.
   Report Maya's absence through November 13 at 17:00. Confirmed work stays intact.
3. **0:55–1:30 — Recovery:** generate three options. Show CP-SAT feasibility, actual
   planned cost, completion, original/result Gantt and protected buffers. Explain
   that options may match when constraints leave the same best feasible plan.
4. **1:30–1:50 — Human approval:** add a decision comment and approve. Show that
   this creates worker confirmation requests, not confirmed calendar assignments.
5. **1:50–2:30 — Worker acceptance:** switch to each worker named in confirmations
   (normally Sofia and Ella). In My invitations, accept the proposed work. Show
   mobile view and the worker's own scoped data.
6. **2:30–3:00 — Publish:** return to Alex → Approval inbox. Revalidate & publish.
   Show published status and the manager/worker calendars. Noah's original work
   remains; Maya's superseded assignment is retained as cancelled history.

For another run: Taylor Chen (administrator) → Adaptive → Reset demo. This removes
fictional proposal/audit state and restores all 12 worker profiles and weather.

Useful failure demonstrations:
- Approve then edit a worker rate/availability or forecast: stale proposal blocks publish.
- Worker declines: revision requested, reservations released, confirmed plan retained.
- Increase contingency or reduce staffing until infeasible: show actual blockers.
- Generate two competing offers: only one overlapping acceptance may reserve time.

Do not claim a live forecast, configured background monitor, measured savings,
validated legal compliance, or hosted Supabase verification during the local demo.
